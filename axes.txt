# One axis per line:   key: Thesis | Antithesis | what the axis is about
# The thesis sits at one end of a rod through the globe's centre, the antithesis at the
# opposite end. Up to 6 axes: 4-6 use the icosahedron's corner pairs (6 lines through the
# centre, every neighbouring pair 63.4 degrees apart), 1-3 use the x, y and z axes (90 degrees
# apart). The description tells Claude what the axis means when it rates each saying.
